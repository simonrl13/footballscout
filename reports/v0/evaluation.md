# Evaluation: 12-month market value change model

All numbers come from [results.md](results.md), which is regenerated by `uv run python -m scout.ml.train`. Values are Transfermarkt crowd estimates, not transfer fees.

## Setup

- **Target:** `log(value_future / value_now)`, where `value_future` is the first valuation 335–425 days after the snapshot.
- **Scope:** 163,902 snapshots from July 2013 onward, where the player's club was in one of 14 European leagues with full match data at the snapshot date (England, Spain, Italy, Germany, France, Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium, Turkey, Russia, Ukraine, Greece, Scotland, Denmark). The other 17 leagues in the dataset only have match data from 2024-25, so they are excluded rather than evaluated on features the model never trained on.
- **Features:** all point-in-time (strictly before the snapshot, apart from the snapshot's own value and club). They are age, current value, 6-month value momentum, minutes over the last 6 and 12 months plus their trend, goal contributions per 90, league at the snapshot date, club points per game over the last 12 months, and position. Contract length was dropped because the data only has each player's *current* contract.
- **Split by snapshot date:** train before 2023-07-01, validation 2023-24, test from 2024-07-01.
  - **Purge:** training rows whose target resolves after 2023-07-01 are removed (125k remain), so no training label contains price moves from the evaluation period.
  - **Validation:** 12,086 rows, used only for early stopping.
  - **Test:** 12,393 rows.
- **Leakage tests:** [tests/test_features.py](../tests/test_features.py) adds data dated on or after the snapshot (valuations, appearances, games) and asserts that the features don't change.

## Results (test set, 2024-25 season)

| Model | MAE | RMSE | Direction acc.* |
|---|---|---|---|
| No change | 0.469 | 0.660 | n/a |
| Age-only (train mean by age) | 0.413 | 0.581 | 70.1% |
| Linear (ridge, same features) | 0.402 | 0.568 | 72.5% |
| **LightGBM** | **0.394** | **0.554** | **73.0%** |

\*Share of snapshots with a non-zero actual change where the predicted and actual signs match.

- LightGBM beats every baseline on every metric, and validation results are nearly identical (MAE 0.388), so the gain is stable across seasons.
- The gain is **modest**. Against no change, MAE drops 16%. Against age alone it drops 5%, and against a linear model on the same features only 2%. Most of the predictable signal is simple: young players rise, players over 28 fall, and expensive players have less room to grow.
- **SHAP importance:** `age` (0.17) > `log_value_now` (0.11) > `value_momentum_6m` (0.08) > `league` (0.05) > playing time and club form (0.02–0.035 each) > position (≤0.01).
- On-pitch performance features add real but small signal, and a 12-month change in a crowd estimate is inherently noisy (a typical miss is ±0.4 in log terms, roughly ±50%).

## Case studies (test set, players valued at €5m or more)

Chosen by rule, not by hand. The three hits are the largest actual moves predicted within 0.1; the two misses are the largest absolute errors.

1. **Hit: João Simões (Sporting CP, 18).** €6m → €15m, actual +0.92, predicted +0.89. Age (+0.43) and a strong club (+0.19) drive the prediction. This was his first valuation in 6 months, so momentum is missing, and LightGBM treats "new to the market" as positive (+0.34).
2. **Hit: Mauro Icardi (Galatasaray, 32).** €9m → €4m, actual −0.81, predicted −0.71. Age (−0.36) and falling momentum (−0.13) add up. This is the classic aging-striker decline.
3. **Hit: Marc Bernal (Barcelona, 17).** €5m → €10m, actual +0.69, predicted +0.77. Age (+0.51) plus a steep recent rise (+0.19).
4. **Miss: Nick Woltemade (Stuttgart, 22).** €7.5m → €70m, actual +2.23, predicted +0.15. A breakout season. The features before December 2024 showed a decent age and some momentum, but nothing that signals a ninefold rise. Moves like this are what the news RAG in Phase 3 is meant to put in context.
5. **Miss: Kirian Rodríguez (Las Palmas, 28).** €12m → €1m, actual −2.48, predicted −0.28. A collapse none of the features hint at: he was playing regularly and his club's form was only mildly negative. Drops this large usually come from events outside the data (injury, health, contract situation).

The pattern is that the model captures the age- and value-driven trend well and misses event-driven shocks. The tails aren't predictable from these features.

## Limitations

- **Scope:** 14 European leagues only. Players at clubs outside them (and in lower divisions) are not covered.
- **Contract length is missing,** and it's probably a major driver of declines near the end of a contract. It would need a point-in-time contract history.
- **Position** uses the player's current position for every snapshot.
- **Minutes** only count competitions in the dataset. A player who recently arrived from an uncovered league shows low minutes.
- **Survivorship bias:** snapshots without a future valuation (retirements, players dropping out of coverage) are excluded, and those skew towards declines.
- **Validation labels** (resolving in 2024-25) overlap the test period. They are only used to choose the number of trees.
- **No hyperparameter tuning** beyond early stopping. The serving model ([models/lgbm.txt](../models/)) is refit on all in-scope labelled data with the early-stopped tree count, so its reported metrics come from the evaluation model, not from it.
